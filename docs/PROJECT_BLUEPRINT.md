# Rivals Original Blueprint

## Core design goals

1. Keep the arena shooter feel fast, readable, and tense.
2. Make the project feel original by emphasizing a distinct visual style and balanced mechanics.
3. Use clean module-based Roblox architecture instead of one giant script.
4. Add future systems for cosmetics, maps, and progression while keeping the base polished.

## Match flow

- Lobby phase: players gather, ready check, map warm-up
- Live phase: active combat, scoring, eliminations
- Round over phase: winner announcement and reset

## Weapon design

- Rifle: balanced all-rounder
- SMG: faster fire, lower damage
- Burst: controlled mid-range pressure
- Shotgun: close-range burst power
- DMR: precise, slower shots for long engagements

## Arena design

- Mid-sized circular arena with open sight lines and cover pillars
- Soft neon accents and darker floor colors for contrast
- Strong readability for enemy movement and kills

## Future expansion ideas

- gun skins and character cosmetics
- more maps and rotating layouts
- ranked ladder and seasonal progression
- sound design and hit feedback
- custom lobby menu and waiting room UI

## Project direction

The next step is to turn this into a polished original game experience with realistic game feel, better textures, and a more premium arena shooter presentation.
